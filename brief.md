# Brief

## Who this site is for, and what they should leave knowing

My portfolio is for product managers and designers interested in how I approach product challenges, and for recruiters evaluating my experience. I want visitors to see how I apply UX and product strategy to hands-on design work across B2B SaaS, fintech, and B2C products. I’m currently pursuing an MSc in Product Management & UX at SKEMA Business School in Paris and Politecnico di Milano in Italy.
